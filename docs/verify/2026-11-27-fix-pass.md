## What the code now enforces

### One renderer and one organ table

- Gameplay draws the mesh pass only. The SDF fullscreen quad is behind
  `PlantRenderer`'s code-level `sdfDevEnabled` (default `false`, not a config
  key); its frame packing, aura and LOD
  updates are no longer called from gameplay. `render.webgpuEnabled` remains the
  WebGPU quarantine switch.
- `packages/shared/src/growth/organTraits.ts` is the single root/soft/woody/
  blade/head and display-order table. Mesh, WebGPU mesh builder, Plant Lab,
  organ shading and the growth cards read it or the shared morphology; no local
  root set or 10-entry card map remains.
- `renderers/plant/organMeshSpec.ts` joins morphology with traits and supplies
  tube sides, cross-section, tip and appendages to the mesh pass. Tube geometry
  now deforms round, ribbed, hollow, flattened and lobed cross-sections, and its
  cache is capped at 1,024 names.
- Morphology stage frames use the same 15/45/80 boundaries as the stage labels.

### Anatomy, joints and materials

- `organAccents.ts` covers all 19 node types, including the seed hilum/seam,
  stem bands/lenticels, branch buds/bark, trunk flare, leaf veins/serration,
  flower sepals/anthers/stigma, spore operculum/annulus, root fissures/
  lenticels, rhizome scales/buds/rootlets and culm sheath/silica band. The
  floating pad's notch is cut from its silhouette rather than drawn on top.
- `socketFillet.ts` builds a swept cone from the parent radius to the child
  radius, with the parent left larger than the child at the socket. Tests cover
  30°, 90° and 150° joints and both thin→fat and fat→thin parents.
- Tubes, joints, sockets, leaves, spines, seeds and ornament children set cast
  and receive shadow flags. Spore and flower heads go through the health/look/
  atmosphere material cache instead of static base materials.

### Picking, disposal and lifecycle

- `MeshPlantRenderer.pickSurfaces()` walks ornament children recursively and
  includes the seed mesh; seed position, pick sphere and gizmo anchor all use
  the stored contact Y plus the shared seed body centre once, not twice.
- Disposal releases seed coat geometry and its hilum/seam children, tooth
  spines,   live ink, ornament children and segment geometry. The tube geometry cache
  and the health/look material-variant cache are bounded (the latter evicts only
  a material no live mesh uses), and the environment disposes its water surface, cave
  texture, aperture volume, cavity bowl and aperture resources. The water
  field's cave texture has a disposal test.

### Pointer, aim and growth cards

- Growth aiming has one pointer path: pointer id ownership, explicit capture,
  second-pointer rejection, `pointercancel`, `lostpointercapture`, window
  `pointerup` and window `blur` cleanup. A direction tap is suppressed so it
  does not also select the part.
- The aim radius is projected from camera-facing right/up axes, not world +X.
  A null hit clears the stale surface offset and anchor. The gene clamp writes
  its clamped yaw/pitch back, so dragging back from the sector edge moves the
  arrow immediately.
- Growth cards are real buttons with `aria-selected`, keyboard focus and a
  labelled `role="group"` panel. The card copy and order are one exhaustive
  table (`ui/growthPartInfo.ts`), tested against the shared display order.

### Root Sight, terrain, camera and water

- A buried eye reserves an envelope slot before roots are counted; if the
  envelope is full, one root slot is taken instead of dropping the eye. The eye
  bubble is skipped when no root opened the cavity. The aperture radius, top
  and bottom come from the same `apertureVolumeFor` result.
- The terrain volume and ground meshes carry `aSurfaceCut`; the authored volume
  uses the soil material and cut attribute rather than an unrelated material.
  The ground census test covers all five ground meshes.
- Free flight keeps its intentional no-clamp path; the ground rig keeps its
  clearance and burrow clamp. Existing camera tests were not replaced.
- Rain is no longer gated by `!eyeUnderSoil`, so an underground eye does not dry
  the surface. The water contour now treats a four-crossing saddle as two wet
  fans when the cell centre is dry, and one polygon when the centre is wet.

### S37

- `uVegetation` is now read in the orbital lowland surface mix.
- The planet detail patch is hidden whenever `planetViewActive`, which removes
  the small bright X-ray speck from under the body.
- Tone is still only mitigated. No screenshot was taken, so no claim is made
  that the final orbital palette reads correctly.

## Not fixed in this pass

- **D01** water is still a surface, **D03** there is no material rendering and
  **D04** there is no 3D flow.
- **U04** free flight through terrain remains intentional, not a bug to clamp.
- **S32** real-phone touch and PWA behaviour are outside code-only proof. The
  pointer work above is desktop and synthetic-event evidence only.
- S37 tone and visual parity need a human visual check.
