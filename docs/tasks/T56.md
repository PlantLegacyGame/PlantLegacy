# T56 — Volumetric Terrain

**Status:** `[~]` **partial — first slice, 2026-11-23.** One authored region is
real 3D volume with an underside, an overhang and strata, meshed from a
signed-distance field by surface nets. The rest of the ground is still the T25
height field; chunk streaming, per-voxel material ids, server collision,
persistence, LOD and the Root Sight interior are **not built**. The row stays
`[ ]`–equivalent (`[~]`) until the acceptance criteria below are met, per the
task rules.

ADR-043 scopes this: the first slice is a single authored region around spawn,
proving the field, the mesher and the materials on real gameplay before
planet-wide generation is attempted. T25's height field remains the surface truth
underneath.

## What changed

| Piece | What it does |
|---|---|
| `packages/shared/src/world/surfaceNets.ts` | Pure surface-nets mesher. One vertex per sign-changing cell, one quad per sign-changing grid edge, gradient normals from the field. Welding is by construction: a cell owns its vertex, so neighbouring triangles cannot crack apart. |
| `packages/shared/src/world/terrainVolume.ts` | The region's signed-distance field: an elliptical rock band standing on its footing, cut to a flat width and cut off `leg_depth_m` below the *local* ground so the feet disappear into the soil. Bed fraction per vertex; region bounds with air on every side; deterministic wobble from the seed. |
| `packages/shared/src/world/terrainVolumeConfig.ts` | Reads `config/terrain_volume.json` through the same config loader the server uses. A missing key throws instead of building a different rock. |
| `apps/client/src/renderers/environment/terrainVolumeMesh.ts` | Turns the mesh arrays into a `BufferGeometry`, paints three shades of the palette's mineral tint per bed (`soil_mineral_tint`), grain deterministic in world position. |
| `EnvironmentRenderer` | Builds the region once at boot, after the ground, against `planetTerrainY` (the drawn surface, not the bare sphere); hides it with the surface shells when the eye is buried. |
| `clientConfig.ts` / `main.ts` | `/config/terrain_volume` is loaded with the other client configs and passed through `EnvironmentTuning.volume`. |

### The field

Negative inside the solid, positive in air. Three cuts intersected with `max`:

- **ring** — distance to the ellipse the band follows, `f / |∇f|`. Its centre is
  the footing, so the lower half is underground and the visible body is an arch.
  Half-thickness is wobbled by two seeded sinusoids, which is what stops it
  reading as a machined pipe.
- **width** — `|w| - width/2`: a flat-sided bridge, not a tube.
- **footing** — `ground(x, z) - leg_depth - y`: closes each foot and keeps the
  buried half of the ellipse from poking out of a hillside.

Footing is the **lowest of the four feet**, so a downhill foot reaches its ground
and an uphill one is buried. There is no plane the field could use instead.

### Measurement

The shipped region, flat ground, this machine: **2 078 vertices, 2 076 quads,
4 152 triangles, 28.5 ms then 35.7 ms** for two builds (Node 24). It is built
once at boot, not per frame. Vertical extent −1.4 m (the foot cut) to +3.65 m
(crown plus half-thickness); **554 vertices face downwards**, which is the
underside the height field could not have.

## 4. Why this shape, and what was cut

- **Not a replacement for the cavity bowl.** The bowl is behind a *dynamic*
  aperture and carries about twenty fixed defects in its rim conform, near-half
  cut, buried-eye sightline and water clip. A static surface-nets mesh cannot be
  cut per frame without re-meshing, and replacing the tuned shell blind - the dev
  browser has not been able to start the game since the last reload - would risk
  all of them. The volume goes where it is additive and verifiable first.
- **Not planet-wide.** ADR-043 says one region. A planet-wide grid at 0.3 m is
  tens of billions of samples.
- **No spurious API.** `isSolidAt`, `columnSpans`, chunk ids, a revision counter
  and a binary snapshot were drafted and dropped: nothing in the game reads them
  yet, and this repo treats a tested-only function as dead code. They arrive with
  the slice that needs them.
- **No second feature.** A stacked shelf would have doubled the field code to
  prove a case the arch already proves (a column with rock above air above
  ground). Caves are the next authored shape.
