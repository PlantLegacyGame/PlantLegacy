## Scope delivered

### Server-side growth simulation (`apps/server/src/ops/plantWorld.ts`)
- `PlantWorld.createPlant()`: creates plant with root node at player spawn position
- `PlantWorld.tryGrow()`: grows a typed node (root/stem/leaf/flower/spore_sac/vine) from parent node, validates biomass cost, layer crossing, growth cooldown, world bounds
- `PlantWorld.step()`: processes upkeep (biomass drain) and resource competition between plants
- ~~`PlantWorld.processRootOverlapDamage()`~~ / ~~`PlantWorld.processRootAttack()`~~: **removed 2026-11-13** (INV-TM-06, ADR-051). Roots contest a tile's water and minerals instead of dealing damage
- `ResourceWorld.updateLight()`: canopy nodes lower light on the tiles below them
- `processGrowthIntents()` in `main.ts`: validates and applies GROWTH_INTENT from clients, broadcasts GROWTH_EVENT with new node
- **Offline simulation tiers** (T03): Active (20 Hz), Near (5 Hz), Dormant (30s steps) with catch-up integration
- **Persistence** (T03): versioned binary snapshot with CRC32, event log, periodic save every 30s, shutdown save

### Client-side growth (`apps/client/src/main.ts`)
- `GROWTH_INTENT` (type 4) sending: grows from selected node in camera forward direction
- `GROWTH_EVENT` (type 16) handling: applies server-broadcast new node to plant node map
- `WelcomePayload` includes full plant node set
- `PlantRenderer.updatePlantData()` receives real plant nodes from server
- Type mapping: root→0, stem→1, vine→2, leaf/flower/spore_sac→3
- **Seeded deterministic RNG** (2026-11-09): `hash01(nodeId, salt)` replaces `Math.random()` for leaf normals and branch curvature

## Addendum 2026-11-16 — what the card promises, and where the tube starts

Two agreement bugs, both of the same kind: the aiming UI and the rules described
different worlds, and the player only found out after committing.

- **Quote = charge (E9, ADR-061, INV-GR-13).** Cards were built from
  `computeGrowthCostQuote`; `tryGrow` then multiplied by soil resistance (~1.75×
  in deep, compacted, dry ground) and by the mutation growth-cost multiplier, and
  divided the time by the mutation growth-speed multiplier. A root card could say
  "6 energy" beside an enabled `Grow` button that the server refused at 11. One
  function, `PlantWorld.growthChargeScale(plant, node, layer)`, now produces the
  scale, `scaleGrowthQuote` applies it to the menu (energy rounded **up**, so a
  card can only be pessimistic), and `tryGrow` charges through the same scale.
  The scale is measured at the part the growth leaves from, because the menu
  cannot know the direction and the soil around the parent is what resistance is
  about. Test: "quotes the soil and mutation charge, so a card never offers
  growth that is refused".
- **Child start = server attach (E10).** The WebGPU mesh pass recomputed a seed
  child's start with `nodeAttachmentPoint` instead of using the `parentAttach*`
  the server shipped, so the stem was drawn from the seed's coat surface while
  the server's segment began 0.3 m lower. Branches, placed at 60 % of that
  segment, hung three centimetres below the drawn stem. Both mesh paths now start
  at the shipped point (the fallback only covers payloads that predate the
  field). Test: `renderers/webgpu/nodeAttachment.test.ts` measures a real grown
  seedling — every child starts on or inside its parent's drawn body, and the
  first internode rises clear of the seed.

Also fixed in the same pass, found by the mutation gallery: the growth-direction
validator's Y bounds had no rounding tolerance while the horizontal bounds did, so
a direction clamped exactly onto `0.65` was refused (`0.6500000000000001 > 0.65`)
— that is why gallery plants were stems with no branches (E7).

## Addendum 2026-11-16 (second batch)

- **A stem grows a stem (E11).** `getAvailableGrowthTypes('stem')` now offers
  `stem`, so a shoot stacks internodes. Before this the only upward organs were
  the mutation-only `trunk` and `culm`, and a `branch` cannot grow another
  branch: an unmutated plant was a 35 cm stump with a fan of leaves off one
  point, and the gallery showed forty-five of them. `trunk`/`culm` remain
  upgrades of that column, not the only way to rise (INV-GR-19).
- **A node's `y` is a height above the soil under that node (INV-GR-18).**
  `completeReroot` re-based the body onto `terrain.heightAt(landing)`; on a 16 m
  rise that put the crown at `y = 16`. Growth, germination and reroot all keep
  the body's own frame now, and the client adds each node's relief when drawing.
  Test: "keeps the body on the ground when it reroots onto a rise".
- **Every node payload is a geometry payload (C10).** `attachFraction` was never
  sent, and `PLANT_CREATED` shipped positions only, so a rival plant (and every
  gallery body) was drawn with guessed joints: mid-segment branches hung off
  their parent's tip. Welcome, sync, growth event and plant-created now carry
  `parentAttach*`, `target*`, `attachFraction` and `radius`.
- **A gallery body is grown from its own seed.** Each mutation's showcase plant
  gets its own azimuth, per-organ scatter, internode count (4–8 plain, 5–7
  woody) and, sometimes, a fork at the base. Same mutation, same plant; different
  mutation, different silhouette.
