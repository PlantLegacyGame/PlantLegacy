# T01 — Procedural plant renderer / Root Sight prototype

**Status:** `[ ]` renderer prototype only. DESIGN.md assigns **T01** to
**Growth** and **C02** to the procedural renderer; this repository committed the
rendering foundation under the T01 label. The growth simulation is not built.
**Phase:** 1 — The Living Sprout. **Depends on:** T00A (per DESIGN.md T01).
**Config owner:** `config/plant_renderer.json` (draft). `config/growth.json` is
still a stub.
**Commits:** `054214b` (renderer + Root Sight), `7d22644` (GLSL3 shader fix).

## Scope delivered

- `config/plant_renderer.json` with 15 values and `GET /config/plant_renderer`.
- `PlantRenderer`: GPU `THREE.Points` buffer, per-node attributes (position,
  normal, biomass, node type, water, nutrients, photosynthesis).
- Root Sight vertex/fragment shaders in GLSL3: root vs canopy coloring, water
  and nutrient flow, photosynthesis visualization.
- Soil ground shader with surface / topsoil / subsoil / bedrock layers, water
  table, root zone and fog.
- Deterministic test-plant generator (mulberry32 seed) for roots and canopy.
- Server cube positions are mapped onto renderer points; a test plant is
  generated on `WELCOME`.

## Manual verification

- Live Pages client loads `/config/plant_renderer` (200) and renders without
  shader errors.
- Local `localhost:4173` shows the layered ground and plant points.
- Root points below `y = 0` are hidden by the opaque ground; the underground
  Root Sight view is not yet visible.

## Known limitations

- **Task-label mismatch:** DESIGN.md T01 is growth; this record covers a
  renderer prototype that belongs closer to C02. Do not treat it as evidence
  that growth exists.
- The test plant is generated on the client, not received from the server.
- Root Sight has no toggle, cutaway sphere, soil transparency or camera mode.
- Many shader colors, generator ranges and `nodeSize` are hardcoded (RULE_3).
- `max_gpu_buffers`, `compute_shader_workgroup_size`, `shader_quality` and the
  spacing/branch values are declared but not all are read by the renderer.
- Roots render behind the opaque ground plane.
