# C03b — Ground LOD and terrain relief

**Status:** implemented 2026-11-13 (part of T25).

## What changed

- The ground reads one shared field (`@plant-legacy/shared/world`), seeded and
  deterministic, with `surface_amplitude_m` 6 m, four octaves and a rim fade.
- `createTerrainPatches` builds two concentric meshes as children of the planet
  body:
  - inner: `terrain_radius` extent at `terrain_mesh_subdivisions` resolution
    (the walkable play area);
  - outer: `patch_radius_m` (620 m default) at a coarser subdivision, carrying
    the same field out to the horizon.
- Each patch is a separate mesh, so a future clipmap can replace the pair
  without changing the field, the collider plan or the camera clamp.
- Physics collides with the exact drawn vertices of both patches as Rapier
  trimeshes. The planet ball stays underneath, so falling through a seam is not
  possible.
- **Patch-local geometry (2026-11-21).** A patch vertex stores its height above
  the pole's tangent plane (curvature drop plus relief, under 30 m over the
  620 m ring) and the mesh is placed at `+radius` inside the body; the soil slab
  does the same, and each Rapier trimesh takes the same placement as a collider
  translation. Storing the radius in the vertices instead spent the whole
  float32 spacing of a 100 km number (7.8 mm) on every height. See
  [planet-scale.md](../planet-scale.md) §3.3 and the P0 record in §6.

## Not yet

- Per-tile clipmap rings. The pair is static, and the `tile_size_m` /
  `tile_vertices` / `lod_distances_m` keys that implied otherwise were deleted
  on 2026-11-21 (P0): there is no reader, so there is no knob.
- Streaming tile swaps and per-tile collider rebuilds.
- Applying server height deltas to the mesh (see T25 known gaps).

## Tests

`apps/client/src/renderers/environment/planetSurface.test.ts` covers sphere
drop, normals, gravity, relief bounds/variation, rim fade, patch cell size,
inner/outer construction, planet continuity and the patch-local vertex space (a
1,240 m patch at radius 100,000 stores under 30 m).
`planetSoilSlab.test.ts` covers wall thickness, the underside, the cavity hole and
the slab's own placement.
