# C03b — Ground detail and terrain relief

**Status:** the current ground is implemented; the continuous detail chain is
not. The old two-patch ground was replaced by the disc/ring/globe described
below. The direction for planet-wide detail, the editable crust and the
planet-scale water/weather blend is in
[planet-scale.md](../planet-scale.md).

## What is built

- One shared terrain field (`@plant-legacy/shared/world`), seeded and
  deterministic, read by the server and the client.
- One `planet-play-disk`: 620 m radius, 2 m cells at the rim, 1,948 angular
  segments and 310 rings, about 1.21 M triangles. It is the only walkable
  ground and has one Rapier heightfield built from the same height function.
- One `planet-horizon-ring`: 620 m to 20 km, 48 quadratic radial rows and the
  disc's angular segments. It starts on the disc rim, blends the local surface
  into `planetReliefAt` over 1.8 km, and ends with an 8 m lip under the globe.
- One `planet-far-relief` globe: 100 km radius, icosphere detail 64, about
  84,500 faces and roughly 1.5 km triangles. It draws the global field at the
  limb and in orbit.
- The globe's `aFarRelief` shader flag cuts its fragments inside an 18 km polar
  arc while the ring is the visible surface. The cut is disabled in planet view
  so the whole globe is drawn; it uses polar arc, not world X/Z, so the south
  pole is not removed.
- `camera_mode` defaults to `ground`; `clampEyeToGround` keeps an eye that is
  already below the soil from staying there unless Root Sight holds a cavity.
- Patch-local metres remain the vertex convention. A vertex stores height above
  the pole tangent plane, and the mesh carries the radius in its transform.

The old square inner/outer pair is gone. The ring is circular and starts
vertex-for-vertex on the disc's rim, so there is no square cap, no hole-cut
sliver and no second LOD boundary inside the walkable area.

## Why it is not detailed everywhere

The disc is 1.21 M triangles over 1.2 km². Repeating that density over the
100 km body would be about 126 billion triangles. The global field is
necessarily coarser, and its shortest octave is 160 m; the local field has
folds down to a few metres. The visible 620 m boundary is a frequency and cell
size boundary, not a player marker. The full arithmetic and the staged fix are
in [planet-scale.md](../planet-scale.md) §1 and §3.

## Not yet

- A continuous view-dependent detail chain. The disc and the 20 km ring are
  still fixed meshes, so the first ring row is ~8.4 m against the disc's 2 m.
- Streaming tile swaps and per-tile collider rebuilds. Physics covers the disc
  only; the rest of the body is scenery.
- A planet-scale high-frequency relief term and global wetness/water tint. The
  local field and the local water solve are richer than the ring's material
  response at the boundary.
- Applying server height deltas to the mesh (see T25 known gaps).
- General cave/overhang streaming; T56's authored arch and aperture soil are
  the first real volumes, not a crust system.

## Tests

`apps/client/src/renderers/environment/planetSurface.test.ts` covers sphere
drop, normals, relief bounds/variation, rim fade, play-disc cell size and rim,
ring construction, globe continuity and patch-local vertex space.
`groundSurfaceCut.test.ts` covers the cut flags on the ground and globe.
`planetSoilSlab.test.ts` covers the slab's walls, underside and cavity hole.
The shader cut for the coarse globe is pinned by
`environment/ground/shaderOverrides.test.ts`.
