# T25 — Terrain, plant colliders, physical dirt

**Status:** core implemented 2026-11-13. `pnpm verify` green.

## What this task does

The ground used to be a smooth sphere plus a single displaced patch, plants had
no colliders, and Root Sight answered "is this buried?" two different ways. T25
gives the world one deterministic ground field, one cavity field, real collider
geometry derived from the drawn skeleton, and sparse soil edits that persist.

## Built

### Shared (`packages/shared`)

- `src/world/terrainField.ts` — integer-hash fBm, `terrainHeightAt`,
  `terrainNormalAt`, `terrainSlopeAt`, `terrainMaterialAt`, rim fade. No THREE,
  no allocation per sample. Same result on server and client from one seed.
- `src/world/cavityField.ts` — clusters underground nodes into ≤N bubbles,
  own-plant priority then a rival budget, polynomial `smin` distance,
  `cavityContains`, `cavityRayRange`, covering sphere.
- `src/physics/collision.ts` — point/segment/capsule/sphere distance math.
- `src/physics/plantColliderPlan.ts` — turns the drawn skeleton into capsules,
  a seed ball and leaf boxes; budget, minimum growth progress and degree-2 chain
  merging. Server gameplay collision and the client's Rapier bodies share it.
- Tests: 130 shared tests pass, including terrain determinism, seam continuity,
  cavity clustering/budget, and collider caps.

### Client

- `renderers/environment/planetSurface.ts` builds an inner detail patch and a
  coarse outer patch from `getTerrainParams()`; both are exact children of the
  planet body, so seams and curvature are shared.
- `renderers/terrain/terrainState.ts` holds the live field parameters loaded
  from `/config/terrain` (plus `soil_depth_m` from `render.json`).
- Physics collides with every drawn patch as a trimesh, with the planet ball
  retained under the relief.
- `renderers/plant/PlantColliders.ts` — one fixed Rapier body per plant,
  capsule per segment, ball for the seed, leaf boxes, group masks
  (ground/plant/debris/probe), nearest 24 plants, 192 colliders per plant,
  merged chains past 70 m, rebuilt at 10 Hz only when a plant's node signature
  changes. Plant↔plant pairs are off; plant↔debris/probe are on.
- Root Sight builds the shared cavity field at `rebuild_hz` (10 Hz), feeds the
  soil cut from its bubbles, and gives plant occlusion the covering sphere.
  Root Sight is a hole the camera may enter, not a burrow it may roam
  (2026-11-18). The free orbit applies only while the eye stands in the
  opening's own column, so a drag down over the pit takes the eye through the
  hole and inside it, with `max_burrow_depth_m` bounding the depth there.
  Anywhere else the ground stops the orbit exactly as with the cutaway shut, and
  an eye that leaves the column underground is lifted back to the ground by
  `clampEyeToGround`.
- The client requests `TERRAIN_REGION` around spawn and while Root Sight is
  open, and folds returned tunnels into the cavity so a restart shows old
  tunnels.

### Server

- `ops/terrain.ts` — sparse chunks (32 × 32 cells of 0.5 m), height delta,
  moisture, compaction and voids. Root growth records a tunnel: volume capped
  by `max_edit_volume_m3`, surface lowering when the run is shallower than
  `surface_break_depth_m` + radius, ring displacement, wall compaction. Voids
  collapse at `collapse_cadence_hz` when dry and loose; backfill loosens the
  soil and dimples the surface. Rain adds moisture, evaporation removes it.
- `growthResistanceAt` multiplies root energy cost and growth time, clamped by
  `growth_resistance_min/max`.
- Persistence: migration `006_create_terrain_tables.sql` (`terrain_chunks`,
  append-only `terrain_edits`, `terrain_voids`) and
  `ops/persistence/terrainPersistence.ts`. Loaded at boot, saved on the plant
  save interval and at shutdown.
- Protocol 32 `TERRAIN_REGION_REQUEST`, 33 `TERRAIN_REGION`, 34
  `TERRAIN_EDITS` (edits broadcast near the new root and replayed on request,
  filtered by distance).

## Known gaps (not done in this pass)

- Height deltas returned by `TERRAIN_REGION` are not yet written into a
  per-chunk R16F texture, so a surface tunnel sink is only visible through the
  Root Sight cavity, not in the daylit ground mesh.
- Debris bodies and rain-drop-on-leaf droop are not wired; the collider groups
  and physics config exist for them.
- The dev overlay does not yet draw chunk bounds, collider wireframes or bubble
  counts.
- Void collapse uses the moisture/compaction stability formula rather than a
  separate `soil_physics` config block; no such block exists yet.
- Client tile-seam and heightfield-vs-mesh tests are covered by the patch unit
  test, not by a headless GPU comparison.
