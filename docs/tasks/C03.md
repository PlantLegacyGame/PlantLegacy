# C03: Environment Rendering

**Status:** Completed
**Scope:** Client-side environment rendering driven by T00A grid and T07 weather / day-night data.
**Owner:** `config/render.json`
**Dependencies:** C01, T07, T00A

---

## 1. Implementation Summary

1. **Continuous Sky Dome & Celestial Arc Grading**:
   - Implemented `EnvironmentRenderer` in `apps/client/src/renderers/EnvironmentRenderer.ts`.
   - Inverted celestial sphere with procedural exponential gradient vertex/fragment shader.
   - Smooth zenith and horizon blending driven by solar elevation ($h = \sin(\text{sunAngle})$):
     - Day, dawn/dusk, and night palettes continuously blended.
     - Directional solar and lunar disks with corona halo glows orbiting in true celestial opposition ($180^\circ$ apart).
     - Full weather state overrides (`clear`, `rain`, `thunderstorm_telegraph`, `thunderstorm_active`, `thunderstorm_aftermath`).

2. **Atmospheric Distance Fog**:
   - Scene fog smoothly lerps both distance boundaries (`fogNear`, `fogFar`) and color tint based on current horizon weather color.
   - Lowers fog horizon during rain and storm conditions for atmospheric immersion.

3. **Lightweight Rain Particle System (Phone Budget Compliant)**:
   - Single `THREE.Points` draw call with 1500 particles contained within camera-following bounding box ($60\text{m} \times 35\text{m}$).
   - Particle positions wrap around camera volume and drop with velocity.
   - Zero CPU/GPU cost when dry (`visible = false`).

4. **Storm Lightning Flashes & Telegraph Cues**:
   - Telegraph phase shifts sky and fog to ominous sulfur-green/slate hue.
   - Active storm triggers periodic random lightning strikes with multi-frame intensity flicker/strobe boosting ambient and directional light by $4.0\times$.
   - Aftermath transitions gracefully with clearing fog and brightening horizon.

5. **Terrain Soil Moisture & Mineral Tint (Root Sight Refinement)**:
   - Ground circle terrain mesh refined to support dynamic soil moisture darkening (mix between dry loam `#4A4536` and wet loam `#1B2014`).
   - Procedural mineral accents based on surface coordinates.
   - Seamlessly preserves and integrates the T02 Root Sight volumetric cutaway shader.
