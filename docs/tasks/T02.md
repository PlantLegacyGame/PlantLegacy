# T02 — Root Sight

**Status:** `[x]` Complete. Two-mode camera, volumetric ground cutaway shader, and root highlight uniforms fully integrated with the Raymarched SDF unified organism renderer (C02). The root system and canopy form one seamless, continuous body through the soil cutaway with local (#4CAF50) and rival (#FF5722) highlight blending.
**Phase:** 1 — The Living Sprout. **Depends on:** T01, C01.
**Config owner:** `config/root_sight.json` (active).

## Scope delivered

### Soil cutaway shader & camera mode (`apps/client/src/main.ts`)
- **Independent camera entity**: camera orbits, zooms, and pans with OrbitControls without affecting the plant.
- **Volumetric cutaway on ground plane**: custom `onBeforeCompile` shader on `groundMaterial` blends soil opacity down to `soilOpacity` (0.3) within `cutawayRadius` around the camera target.
- **Smooth transition**: linear progress state updated with delta time over `transitionMs` (500 ms default), eased using cubic ease-out `1 - (1 - t)^3`. Reverses cleanly mid-transition.
- **Grid helper fade**: line opacity reduces smoothly during transition to avoid visual clutter underground.
- **Local-only state**: toggling Root Sight does not send network messages or alter server state. Other players cannot detect when a player is in Root Sight (RULE_4, RULE_27).
- **Target reference**: defaults to `own_plant` (centers cutaway sphere on root node at y=0) with support for `camera_position`.

### Root shader visualization (`apps/client/src/renderers/PlantRenderer.ts`)
- **Direct SDF Evaluation:** Root branches are evaluated as subterranean capsule primitives ($y < 0$) within the unified organism distance field. The taproot, root crown at $y=0$, and trunk form one seamless physical entity.
- **Shader uniforms**: `uRootSightEnabled`, `uRootSightProgress`, `uRootSightSphereCenter`, `uRootSightSphereRadius`, `uOwnRootHighlightColor`, `uRivalRootHighlightColor`.
- **Root differentiation via `smin` blend**: Primitive properties encode `isOwnPlant`. Inside the cutaway radius, roots smoothly highlight in `uOwnRootHighlightColor` (viewer's own roots) or `uRivalRootHighlightColor` (rival roots) with organic color transitions.
- **Surface tubes unaffected**: Above-ground canopy/stem tubes and leaves maintain standard lighting and materials.

### Config and server route (`config/root_sight.json`, `apps/server/src/main.ts`, `apps/client/src/config/clientConfig.ts`)
- `config/root_sight.json`: active extended-1 schema config with cutaway radius, transition ms, soil opacity, highlight colors, substrate multiplier, and camera target.
- `apps/server/src/main.ts`: serves `GET /config/root_sight` reading runtime config with fallbacks.
- `apps/client/src/config/clientConfig.ts`: `loadRootSightConfig` loads and normalizes snake_case to camelCase.
- `apps/client/src/main.ts`: subscribes `root_sight_toggle` action from `config/controls.json` (`Tab` key).

## Config: `config/root_sight.json` (status `active`)

| Key | Type | Range | Default | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `cutaway_radius` | number | 5–500 | 50.0 | Radius of underground cutaway sphere |
| `transition_ms` | integer | 0–3000 | 500 | Transition duration between modes (ms) |
| `soil_opacity` | number | 0.0–0.9 | 0.3 | Opacity of soil inside the cutaway |
| `own_root_highlight_color` | color | — | `#4CAF50` | Highlight color for local player's roots |
| `rival_root_highlight_color` | color | — | `#FF5722` | Highlight color for rival players' roots |
| `substrate_multiplier` | number | 0.1–1.0 | 0.5 | Physics movement multiplier for uprooted crawl (T06) |
| `camera_target` | enum | own_plant, camera_position | `own_plant` | Center reference for cutaway sphere |

5. In Client 2, verify Client 1's roots are visible; toggle `Tab` on Client 2 and observe Client 1's roots highlight in orange/red (`#FF5722`) while Client 2's own roots highlight in green.
6. Verify neither client broadcasts any packet on `Tab` toggle (Network tab shows no WebSocket messages for toggle).
7. Orbit camera with mouse: camera moves smoothly around plant; plant position does not change.
8. Press `Tab` again → smooth ease-out closes the cutaway back to standard surface rendering.
