# T02 — Root Sight

**Status:** `[x]` Complete. Two-mode camera with subterranean elevation, organic root-envelope ground cutaway shader, and natural botanical subterranean visualization fully integrated with the plant rendering pipeline (C02 WebGL SDF & C02b WebGPU). Underground roots maintain their natural botanical colors, revealed through smooth organic cavities ("air bubbles") carved along underground growths.
**Phase:** 1 — The Living Sprout. **Depends on:** T01, C01.
**Config owner:** `config/root_sight.json` (active).

## Scope delivered

### Soil cutaway shader & camera mode (`apps/client/src/main.ts`, `ground.ts`, `EnvironmentRenderer.ts`)
- **Independent camera entity**: camera orbits, zooms, and pans with OrbitControls without affecting the plant.
- **Subterranean camera tilt**: when Root Sight is active, `maxPolarAngle` smoothly extends to `Math.PI - 0.05`, allowing players to tilt the camera beneath the soil line ($y < 0$) to inspect root geometry from underground angles.
- **Organic envelope cutaways ("air bubbles" through soil)**: ground cutaways are not a generic flat circle and do not follow the camera; the soil shader evaluates a smooth minimum distance (`smin`) across all subterranean root nodes of player and rival plants (`uRootSightNodes[64]` / `uRootSightNodeCount`, uploaded each frame from `plantNodes` and `otherPlayerPlants`). Dirt opens up along the actual branching paths of underground growths as one organic cavity, with a smooth alpha-blended rim and earthen rim shading that darkens the soil just outside the cavity.
- **3D air bubble, not a 2D cutout**: the soil shader itself marches the void (`rootSightSdf` / `rootSightInteriorWall` in `ground.ts`). From the eye the ray enters the fused node set at the cut and exits on the far side; that exit point is shaded as the bubble's **interior wall**, with its own clod noise, sun term and a depth ramp measured in bubble radii. Because it is a world position it moves with the camera, so oblique and underground views get real parallax. There is no cavity mesh, no backdrop sphere and no second surface: one surface supplies the opening, the dirt behind it and the rim, so nothing can leak sky between two geometries.
- **Per-node dimensions**: each node uploads its own bubble radius in `uRootSightNodes[i].w`, scaled from that node's own `radius` relative to the largest underground node and capped by `cutaway_radius`. Any underground element (seed, radicle, root, lateral root, own or rival) therefore carries its own Root Sight bubble, and the set is fused with `smin` into one blended cavity.
- **No soil dome**: the old `soilDome` hemisphere and the WebGPU `dome.wgsl` bowl were removed; their geometry rings were the concentric circles seen under the surface.
- **Smooth transition**: linear progress state updated with delta time over `transitionMs` (500 ms default), eased using cubic ease-out `1 - (1 - t)^3`. Reverses cleanly mid-transition.
- **Automatic activation on subterranean growth**: selecting a subterranean growth option (radicle, root, lateral root) or focusing on a subterranean node automatically engages Root Sight and centers the camera on the target node.
- **Local-only state**: toggling Root Sight does not send network messages or alter server state. Other players cannot detect when a player is in Root Sight (RULE_4, RULE_27).

### Root shader visualization (`PlantRenderer.ts`, `common.wgsl`, `mesh.wgsl`, `WebGPURenderer.ts`)
- **Natural botanical coloration**: roots underground retain their natural bark and root colors under regular Root Sight; visibility is achieved organically by peeling back soil envelopes rather than neon recoloring.
- **Subterranean node aura buffer**: passes up to 64 underground node positions and radii via 1D texture (`rootAuraTexture`) to both WebGPU and WebGL pipelines for unified distance evaluation.
- **Multi-node support**: tracks all underground growths across player and rival plants for complete subterranean situational awareness.

### Config and server route (`config/root_sight.json`, `apps/server/src/main.ts`, `apps/client/src/config/clientConfig.ts`)
- `config/root_sight.json`: active extended-1 schema config with cutaway radius, transition ms, soil opacity, highlight colors, substrate multiplier, and camera target.
- `apps/server/src/main.ts`: serves `GET /config/root_sight` reading runtime config with fallbacks.
- `apps/client/src/config/clientConfig.ts`: `loadRootSightConfig` loads and normalizes snake_case to camelCase.
- `apps/client/src/main.ts`: subscribes `root_sight_toggle` action from `config/controls.json` (`KeyR`; the binding moved off `Tab` when the browser started using it for focus traversal).

## Manual verification

## Addendum 2026-11-13 (T25): one cavity field

Root Sight's bubbles now come from `packages/shared/src/world/cavityField.ts`
instead of a per-node loop written in `main.ts`. The field clusters underground
nodes (own plant first, rivals within 40 m and capped by
`rival_bubble_budget`), buries bubbles by `bubble_merge_cell_m`/
`bubble_padding_m` rules, and produces a covering sphere. The soil cut, plant
occlusion and camera all use that one aperture; the WebGPU frame still carries a
single sphere rather than the bubble array, so a fragment between two bubbles
can be treated as visible. This is a deliberate conservative bound, documented
in `docs/tasks/T25.md`.

The camera no longer clamps the eye above the ground while Root Sight is open;
it may follow roots down to `max_burrow_depth_m` and is clamped back above the
surface when Root Sight closes. (The above-ground floor is
`camera_ground_touch_m` since 2026-11-17 — a contact epsilon, not a stand-off.)

## Addendum 2026-11-16: the cavity is cut in both faces

The soil is a `THREE.DoubleSide` skin, and the aperture discard was gated on
`gl_FrontFacing`. From outside and from above the pit read correctly, but from
inside the ground `cutMask` was 0 for every back face, so nothing was discarded
and the earth stayed a closed ceiling over the cavity: Root Sight could only be
seen from outside and from above. The aperture is now cut on both faces. The
earthen lip beside the cut stays a surface-only effect and is faded out with the
eye under the soil (`lip * (eyeIsUnder ? 0.0 : 0.55)`), which is what the
front-face gate was originally there for. `environment/groundCutaway.test.ts`
fails if the cut is ever put back behind `gl_FrontFacing`.

The cavity also follows the body rather than the mirror's iteration order: the
anchor is the server's `rootNodeId`, then a seed, then the crown position the
server streams with every uproot transform (`player/ownPlantAnchor.ts`). A
recentre (Space) uses the same anchor and filters the mirror to this plant's
node ids.

## Addendum 2026-11-16 — the view opens itself, and the cavity opens with it

Root Sight is still player-owned (`R`), but it no longer waits to be found.

- **Auto-open on buried growth.** `player/rootSightAutoOpen.ts` decides it:
  a part that starts growing underground (type or plane) opens the view if it is
  not already open or opening. It fires once per new buried part, so a manual
  close stands until the player grows something else underground, and
  `root_sight.auto_open_on_root_growth` turns the rule off. Called from
  `beginGrowthAiming` (choosing a root option) and from the local branch of the
  `GROWTH_EVENT` handler.
- **The cavity opens at the first frame of growth.** The bubble builder skipped
  nodes under 2% grown and scaled the bubble radius by the growth fraction, so
  the first seconds of a new root had nothing to cut with. A starting part now
  contributes a bubble floored at 25% of its own radius, which widens as it grows.
- **The client finally receives the whole file.** `/config/root_sight` was a
  seven-key whitelist; it is now served by the flat generic route, so the cutaway
  radius, bubble budget, burrow depth and rebuild rate in
  `config/root_sight.json` are the ones the cavity runs on.

Verification: `player/rootSightAutoOpen.test.ts`; `pnpm dev`, grow a radicle and
watch the ground open without pressing `R`; press `R` to close, grow another root
and watch it open again.

## Addendum 2026-11-16 (second batch)

- **Visibility follows the opening, in volume (A13, INV-RS-14/15, ADR-064).**
  The plant pass hid any fragment the planet sphere stood in front of, in one
  sphere around the crown. A deep root inside the hole was hidden, and a camera
  that flew down into the cavity was *inside* the planet, so every root vanished:
  the view from in there was a brown wall. Now:
  - above ground the test is a **cylinder the size of the aperture**, because the
    soil is cut in plan view — every root under the hole is exposed at any depth,
    and nothing outside it leaks through dirt;
  - a **buried eye** (`WebGPURenderer.setCameraUnderSoil`) skips the surface test,
    since dirt between it and a root is the medium it stands in;
  - a buried eye gets an **air bubble** (`root_sight.eye_window_radius_m`, default
    1.1 m) fused into the same envelope as the roots, and `ground.ts` cuts that
    envelope — not just the aperture — while the eye is under the soil. The
    window is only added when the cavity is already open, so a buried camera
    cannot dig for the plant.
- **The sheets end radially (W4).** Both water grids (and the damp-ground stain)
  fade with distance from the camera *and* with distance from the sheet centre
  (`sheet_edge_fade`), so a camera high enough to see the whole grid no longer
  sees water drawn as a square.
