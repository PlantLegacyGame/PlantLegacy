# Compliance policy

**Status:** drafted by T00. Enforced by **T40** (weeks 64–65).
**Machine-readable copy:** `config/compliance.json` → `values.policy_lines`.

This is a five-line illegal-content-only policy, required to keep payment rails
and hosting alive. It is a compliance floor, not moderation (RULE_6, RULE_12).

## The policy

1. We remove content that depicts or facilitates child sexual abuse material.
2. We remove content that contains a credible threat of violence against a person.
3. We remove content that publishes another person's private personal information.
4. We remove content that facilitates illegal activity.
5. Nothing else is removed for content reasons; offensive, political, weird and
   hostile expression stays.

## Scope

Applies to: chat, profiles, trade listings, direct messages, guild names and
mottos, mail, reviews.

## What this policy does not do

- It does not rate, rank or hide content for being unpleasant.
- It does not create a general moderation queue.
- It does not tell the reporter what action was taken.
